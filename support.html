Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device and in your own private iCloud account. We don't have servers and can't see your data.

My data isn't syncing between devices.

Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID on each device, iCloud is turned on for Pluno7 in iOS Settings, and you have iCloud storage available.

How do I stop sharing a project or account?

Open the shared project or account in Pluno7 and use its sharing options to stop sharing.

How do I delete my data?

Delete entries in the app. To remove synced data, go to iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Pluno7.
