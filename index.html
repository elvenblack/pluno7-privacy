Privacy Policy for Pluno7

Effective date: 10/17/2026 Developer: ACACIASOFT Contact: apple.developer@acacia-soft.com

Pluno7 is a personal expense tracker. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: we don't collect your data. Your financial information stays on your device and in your own iCloud account. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

What Pluno7 stores

Everything you enter in the app, including:

  • Expenses, income, categories, budgets and recurring expenses
  • Accounts (bank, cash, credit card, loan, savings, crypto) and their balances, goals and statements
  • Projects and tags
  • Crypto holdings (the coins and quantities you record)
  • Your display name and app settings (theme, currency, app lock)

This information is stored on your device. We never receive a copy of it.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Pluno7 syncs your data between your own devices using Apple's CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. It is protected by your Apple account and is subject to Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot see, access or recover this data. If you are not signed in to iCloud, your data stays on your device only.

Deleting the app from a device does not delete data already synced to iCloud. You can remove it in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Sharing

Pluno7 lets you share a project or an account with other people using Apple's CloudKit sharing. When you invite someone, the items you choose to share (for example, the expenses and income in a shared account) become visible to the people you invite, and they can contribute as you allow. Sharing is always your choice, and you can stop sharing at any time. People you share with are identified through their Apple ID; we do not see who they are.

Information sent to third parties

  • Crypto prices. If you add a crypto account, Pluno7 requests current and historical prices from CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com). Each request contains the identifier of the coin (for example "bitcoin") and the currency you display. It does not contain your name, balances, holdings or any other personal data. As with any internet request, CoinGecko can see your device's IP address and handles it under its own privacy policy. If you don't use crypto accounts, Pluno7 makes no such requests.

Pluno7 contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools, and it does not sell or share your data.

Camera and receipt scanning

If you scan a receipt, Pluno7 uses the camera and Apple's on-device text recognition (Vision) to read the amount and details. Scanning happens on your device; the image is not sent to us or to any third party.

Face ID and app lock

If you turn on app lock, Pluno7 uses Face ID or Touch ID through Apple's system authentication. Your biometric data is handled entirely by iOS. Pluno7 only receives a yes or no result and never has access to your biometric data.

Notifications

Pluno7 can send reminders for upcoming payments and recurring expenses. These are scheduled on your device. Pluno7 also uses silent notifications from Apple so that shared items can update. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

Children

Pluno7 is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to delete.

Your choices and control

  • Edit or delete any entry in the app at any time.
  • Turn off iCloud for Pluno7 in iOS Settings to stop syncing.
  • Stop sharing any shared project or account from within the app.
  • Delete the app to remove locally stored data.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Pluno7 handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
